About Joylight Oil & Gas LTD

Joylight Oil & Gas LTD is a distinguished Nigerian company, proudly incorporated under the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Abuja, in accordance with the Companies and Allied Matters Act of 1990. We specialize in delivering premium oil and gas well-servicing solutions, focusing on three core areas:

  • Lift-Barge Services.
  • Wire-Line Services.
  • Supply of Down-Hole Production Tools.

Strategically headquartered in Nigeria, the hub of oil and gas operations in Tropical West Africa, Joylight Oil & Gas LTD capitalizes on the dynamic growth opportunities within the sector. By maintaining a sharp competitive edge, we consistently deliver exceptional value to our clients while driving innovation in the industry. Our extensive operations span across Nigeria and West Africa, providing cutting-edge solutions and unparalleled expertise in heavy equipment, mechanical, and technical services. Our commitment to quality and innovation ensures that our clients benefit from reliable, efficient, and customized solutions tailored to their unique requirements. At Joylight Oil & Gas LTD, we proudly serve commercial, governmental, and industrial markets, with particular emphasis on the power generation and petroleum refinery sectors. Guided by our unwavering dedication to excellence and progress, we continue to solidify our reputation as a trusted leader in the energy landscape of West Africa.
